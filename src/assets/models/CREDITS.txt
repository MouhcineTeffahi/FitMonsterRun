All 3D models in this folder are free for use as noted below.

Kenney (www.kenney.nl) - Food Kit 2.0 — CC0
  burger (burger-cheese), donut (donut-sprinkles), fries, soda,
  broccoli, chicken (meat-cooked), apple, protein (carton, unused in-run)
  Textures were embedded into each .glb with gltf-transform.

protein-tub.glb <- "Protein Powder" by Zsky on Poly Pizza
  https://poly.pizza/m/w9sWcWlV9i — Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY)
pedestrians.glb <- Kenney (www.kenney.nl) "Mini Characters" — CC0
  12 characters (male/female a–f) with walk, sprint, fall and emote-yes clips,
  merged into one file by scripts/build-pedestrians.mjs.

fit-monster.glb <- Quaternius "Universal Base Characters" Superhero Male (CC0),
  restyled by scripts/build-character.mjs into the Fit Monster: smoothed mask
  face, almond eyes, "N" chest harness, wristbands, shorts with piping,
  knee leggings, yellow socks and sneakers baked as geometry with per-region ids.
fit-monster-anims.glb <- Quaternius "Universal Animation Library" (CC0):
  Sprint_Loop, Jump_*, Idle_Loop, Dance_Loop, Roll, Crouch_Fwd_Loop,
  Death01, Hit_Chest.
  Both mirrored at codeberg.org/jamesonBradfield/Quaternius_IK_Rigged_with_animations.

Everything else in the world (trucks, road, buildings, palms, street props,
water bottle, dumbbells) is procedural geometry built in src/game/. Sound effects
in src/assets/sounds are synthesised by scripts/make-sfx.mjs (original).

src/assets/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf <- Lilita One by Juan Montoreano
  (Google Fonts), SIL Open Font License 1.1, see src/assets/fonts/OFL.txt.
